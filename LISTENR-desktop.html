Our manifesto

Everyone deserves to be heard.

We live in a world where everyone has something to say, but finding someone who will simply listen is becoming harder than it should be.

We’re constantly expected to keep up, make decisions, hold things together and get on with life. We learn to put difficult feelings aside, convince ourselves that things aren’t so bad, or wait until we have the right words to explain what’s going on.

Sometimes, we don’t even know what’s wrong. We just know that something feels heavy.

And sometimes, we don’t need advice. We don’t need someone to tell us what to do, compare our experiences with theirs, or remind us to look on the bright side.

We just need someone to listen.

That’s why we exist.

We believe that being heard shouldn’t depend on who you know, how well you communicate, or whether your problems seem big enough to deserve attention.

You don’t have to pretend you’re okay here. You don’t have to make sense. You don’t have to arrive with a plan or leave with an answer.

You can talk about your career, your relationships, your worries, the things you haven’t told anyone, or absolutely nothing in particular.

We’ll make the space for it.

What we promise:

  • NOjudgement.
  • NOunsolicited advice.
  • NOpressure to explain yourself.
  • NOexpectation to have everything figured out.
  • NOneed to pretend you’re doing fine.

Because life is complicated enough.And nobody should have to carry everything on their own.

FAQ

Questions, answered gently

What does a session feel like?
You do most of the talking. Your listener doesn’t interrupt with advice or their own stories; they help you untangle your thoughts, so you leave clearer and lighter.
How many sessions do I need?
As many or as few as you like. Many people book one when something feels heavy.
How qualified are listeners?
Listeners are trained in active, non-judgmental listening. Placeholder copy for the prototype.
Are sessions confidential?
Yes. Calls are private and never recorded, and you can stay anonymous.
What if I’m in crisis?
Listenr isn’t an emergency service. Please call 112, or Tele-MANAS on 14416, any time.
How is it different from therapy?
Listening sessions don’t diagnose or treat. They give you room to be heard. If you need therapy, a listener can gently say so.
