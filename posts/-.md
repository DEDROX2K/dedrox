As a daily Chicago CTA rider, I wanted to track my own transit data accurately, but nothing existed to do it. So I built Transit Stats, the first app designed to turn public transit rides into meaningful personal analytics. From concept to App Store launch in four months, I designed the brand, all the UI/UX, and website while writing the backend code as well as the frontend code with the help of AI tools.

## 1. Challenge
Public transit generates an enormous amount of data, yet almost none of it ever reaches the rider. As a daily CTA rider, I had no way to track my trips, measure my environmental impact, know if my monthly pass was worth it, or understand my habits over time. I wanted to take Chicago’s overlooked transit data and make it personal, useful, and fun.

## 2. Who I Designed For
I designed for two types of riders. One is drawn from my friends who commute on the CTA daily, and the other is me, always exploring new areas of the city.


| ### The Daily Commuter                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | ### The City Explorer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| - Rides the same lines from the same stops on a regular basis<br>    <br>- Wants to understand their habits: time, distance, and most-used lines<br>    <br>- Wants their daily commute to add up to something visible<br>    <br>- Interested in baseline stats, leaderboard, and environmental impact | <br><br>- Always going new places, riding new routes and visiting new stops<br>    <br>- Treats the transit map like a checklist to complete<br>    <br>- Wants credit for every new stop and line they reach<br>    <br>- Interested in total coverage, achievements, and leaderboards |
