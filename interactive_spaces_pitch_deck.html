01The problem and the idea

Why make spaces interactive?

Use the switch to see the challenge, then how we approach it.

The challenge

A quiet retail, public or visitor space with a screen people are walking past

01 / The problem

Most screens are easy to walk past.

A display can show information, but that does not always give people a reason to stop, explore or take part.

The opportunity

An interactive display with people using it together

02 / The idea

Give people something to take part in.

We bring displays, cameras and software together to create experiences people can play with, learn from and remember.
