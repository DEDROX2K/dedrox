Interactive kiosks to immerse your customers.

Why make your shop interactive?

Purchase decisions are often influenced by the environment and the experiences people have while shopping.

Most screens are easy to walk past and they all show boring advertisements.

A display can show information, but that does not always give people a reason to stop, explore or take part.

02 / The idea

Give people something to take part in.

We bring displays, cameras and software together to create experiences people can play with, learn from and remember.

02What we do

- Reduce the time to purchase!

- Premium interactions that drive engagement and sales.

- Interactive way to increase visibility & stand out from your competitors.

Something different in a sea full of sameness!

01

Interactive games

Multiplayer, motion- and touch-driven games built on Unity, Unreal or WebGL, scaling from a single kiosk to a 10m video wall.

  • Skeletal and gesture tracking via depth sensors
  • 2–40 concurrent players per installation
  • Sub-80ms input-to-screen latency
  • Live leaderboards and session analytics
02

Brand experiences

Branded touch and motion interfaces triggered by RFID, NFC or QR, with content swapped remotely through our CMS.

  • RFID / NFC / QR trigger points
  • Remote content scheduling and A/B variants
  • Dwell-time and completion-rate reporting
  • White-labelled to brand design systems
03

Product exploration

3D product configurators rendered in WebGL or Three.js, pulling live inventory and pricing through a REST API.

  • Real-time 3D configuration and material swaps
  • Live inventory and pricing via REST/GraphQL
  • Companion mobile AR preview
  • CRM and lead-capture integration
04

Learning and entertainment

Multi-touch tables and walls with adaptive content difficulty, built for long, repeated use in museums and visitor centres.

  • Up to 32-point PCAP multi-touch
  • Adaptive difficulty and spatial audio
  • High-contrast, voice-narration and switch-access modes
  • Usage logs for curators and exhibit teams
03The technology

The experience is only one part of the job. It also needs reliable hardware, responsive tracking and software that is straightforward to manage.

01

Displays and hardware

The screens, kiosks and equipment that bring an installation to life.

  • 55"–98" commercial LED / LCD panels, 500–2500 nit
  • IP54-rated enclosures for outdoor and semi-outdoor sites
  • PoE++ powered, 24/7 duty-cycle rated
  • Embedded Android/Linux player with remote MDM and OTA updates
02

Depth cameras

Cameras that can read movement and help an experience respond to people.

  • Time-of-flight and stereo depth sensing, up to 5m range
  • 30–60fps skeletal tracking, multi-person segmentation
  • On-device edge inference, under 50ms response
  • Stable across variable ambient light conditions
03

Software platform

The software used to run experiences, manage content and review activity.

  • Real-time engine: Unity, Unreal or WebGL
  • Cloud CMS for scheduling, remote deploys and content swaps
  • REST and WebSocket APIs for third-party integration
  • Analytics: dwell time, heatmaps, conversion funnels
  • Offline-first caching with automatic sync on reconnect
