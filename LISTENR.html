FAQ

Questions, answered gently.

What does a session feel like?

A focused space where you do about 80% of the talking. Your listener doesn’t interrupt with advice or their own stories; they help you untangle your thoughts.

Hearing your words reflected back helps you clear your head and leave feeling lighter.

How many sessions do I need?

As many or as few as you like. Many people book a single session when something feels heavy.

How qualified are listeners?

What if I’m in crisis?

Listenr isn’t an emergency service. Please contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline right away.
