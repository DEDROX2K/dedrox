What does a session feel like?
You do about 80% of the talking. Your listener doesn’t interrupt with advice or their own stories; they help you untangle your thoughts, so you leave clearer and lighter.
How many sessions do I need?
As many or as few as you like. Many people book one when something feels heavy.
